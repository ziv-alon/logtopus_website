Core Architectural Principles:

1. Homogeneous SKU Storage Stacks: Each vertical stack in the storage cube holds a single product SKU (uniform color per stack, distinct color per stack), eliminating bin reshuffling ("zero digging").

2. Multi-SKU Order Consolidation on Pallets: Stacks on the staging pallets represent outbound customer shipments composed of multiple products—every box on a pallet has its own distinct product color.

3. Orthogonal Rail Navigation & Hoist Delivery: Articulated overhead robots travel strictly along X/Y ceiling rails and lower picked SKUs directly onto the multi-product shipment pallets.
